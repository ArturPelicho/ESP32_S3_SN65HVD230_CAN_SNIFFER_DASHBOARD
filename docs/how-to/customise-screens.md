# Lay out your screens

The Map and the Dashboard are built from **elements** you choose and place yourself.

![The Dashboard tab](../img/app/dashboard.png)

## Pick what shows

- **Map**: ☰ → **On screen**.
- **Dashboard**: the ⚙ in its corner.

Under **Shown on this screen**, switch elements on or off: speed (with the limit sign), gear,
RPM, rev counter, shift lights, fuel tank, range, head temperature, consumption now and
average, clock, bike and GPS link, battery, intake air, throttle, trips A and B, odometer,
coordinates, altitude, engine faults, north, lean and pitch, and on the map the track
progress bar. Landscape and portrait each keep their own choice.

## Place and size them

1. Switch on **Move mode**.
2. Drag an element onto another slot. Dropping it on another element swaps the two.
3. **Save**, or **Revert** to undo.

**Slot size** (Small, Medium, Large) sets how big the grid is, and **Background opacity** how
much of the map shows through. **Reset layout** goes back to the start.

## The map's button bar

In ☰ → **On screen** → **Button bar**: put it on any edge of the screen and hide the buttons
you never use. The ☰ button always stays.

## Lean gauge

The **Lean / pitch** element shows the phone's angle. With the bike upright and level, long
press it to set zero.

# Calls and messages

Settings → Phone → **Calls and messages**.

## Calls

1. Switch on **Show calls with answer / reject buttons** and allow phone access when asked.
2. An incoming call shows as one row with big **Answer** and **Reject** buttons, sized for
   gloves. Once answered, a slim bar shows the call time and **Hang up**.

## Messages

1. Switch on **Show messages** and tap **Allow notification access**: Android opens its list of
   apps that may read notifications; switch NavExpress on there.
2. Pick the apps under **Messages from**: SMS, WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, Messenger and others.
3. Choose whether to **Show the message text** and to **Read messages aloud**.

Calls and messages appear away from the navigation boxes, so the next turn stays in view.

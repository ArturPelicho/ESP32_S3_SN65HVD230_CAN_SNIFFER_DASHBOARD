# Record a ride

1. Tap **REC** on the button bar. It turns into the distance ridden so far.
2. Ride. Recording carries on with the screen off (allow location **all the time**, see
   [Getting started](../getting-started.md#keep-it-running-with-the-screen-off)).
3. Tap it again → **Stop and save**. The ride is saved in Settings → Tracks → Routes, with
   times. Rides under 100 m are not kept.

**Automatically**: Settings → Tracks → Recording → **Start when the bike connects**. Recording
then starts when the bike unit connects and stops 15 minutes after the bike goes off.

Share a ride as GPX from its menu like any other track. Ridden again in Track mode, a recorded
ride brings its own [section times](pacenotes.md#section-times) as the bests to beat.

## Measure and draw

- **Measure distance from here** (long press the map): an orange cross sits in the middle of
  the screen; slide the map and long press to add points. The bar shows the total.
- **Direct line to here** (long press the map): a line from the bike with its distance and
  bearing, kept until you remove it (long press its end).

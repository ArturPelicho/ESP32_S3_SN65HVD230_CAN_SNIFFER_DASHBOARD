# Ride with pacenotes and section times

In **Track** mode NavExpress reads the road ahead like a rally co-driver, from the shape of
the track itself.

![Pacenotes in Track mode](../img/app/pacenotes.jpg)

## Turn it on

1. Follow a track ([how](follow-a-track.md)).
2. Long press the track box and choose **Track (fixed line)**.
3. In the same menu, check **Pacenotes in Track mode** is on.

## What you hear

- Each corner is graded from **1** (tightest) to **6** (almost straight) by its radius, plus
  **hairpin**, **long**, **tightens** and **opens**.
- **Crest** and **dip** from the terrain heights.
- Notes close together are linked ("into", "into a counter-curve"), and each call ends with
  the straight to the next note, rounded the rally way: 30, 50, 70, 100, 150…
- Prefer numbers? **Pacenotes as numbers (1–6)** says "long right 3 tightens" instead of
  "long sharp right, tightens".
- **Pacenote lead** sets how early the next call comes (picked from a list); a call never
  starts before you leave the previous corner.

## Section times

Every track is cut into sections of about a kilometre, each ending after a corner. At the end
of a section the app says **Fast** or **Slow** against your best time there, and shows the
difference. Bests are kept per track and per direction. A ride you recorded with the app
starts with its own times as the first bests.

Switch them with **Section times** in the track box menu.

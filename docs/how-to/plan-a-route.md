# Plan a route on the map

Draw a day out with as many stops as you like. The legs follow the roads.

## Draw it

1. Long press where the route starts and choose **Start a new route here**.
2. Long press the next place and choose **Add waypoint 2 to …**. Carry on for each stop; the
   waypoints are numbered in order.
3. Each leg follows the roads with the [route type](route-types.md) you chose. With the
   region's offline routing it works without signal.

## Change it

- **Long press a waypoint**: move it (drag the map under the cross, then **Place here**) or
  delete it.
- **Long press the line**: insert a waypoint there, continue the route from its end, rename
  it, change its colour and width, save a copy, or delete it.

## Ride it

Long press the line → **Follow this route**. The track box shows the arrival time at the next
waypoint and at the end.

## Keep and share it

Everything you draw is kept in the app straight away (Settings → Tracks → Routes). From the
line's menu, **Share GPX** sends it to another app and **Save GPX to the phone** writes the file.

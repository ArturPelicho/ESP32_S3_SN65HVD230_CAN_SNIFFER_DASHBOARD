# Set the map's look and detail

![Map settings](../img/app/map-settings.png)

## Day and night

Settings → Preferences → **Theme**: **Sand** or **Graphite** by day; warm **desert**,
**Graphite** or pure-black **OLED** by night. It switches by itself at sunset where you are,
from the light sensor or with the phone's dark mode; or set it by hand. The **DAY / NIGHT**
button on the map switches it on the spot.

![Night map in 3D](../img/app/nav-night.png)

## The view

☰ → **View**: heading up (▲), **3D**, or north up (N); the same button on the bar switches it.
**Auto zoom** zooms in in town and out on the open road; set the speeds and zooms it uses with
the two sliders (the slow, closer end is on the left).

## Layers

Tap the layers button. Tap a layer to switch it, and long press one marked ⋯ for its options.

![Layers](../img/app/layers.png)

| Layer | Options |
|---|---|
| Tracks on the map | Hide the followed line while navigation carries on |
| Shaded relief | Strength |
| Contour lines | Spacing for the zoom you are at; each zoom keeps its own |
| Roads | Each road class on or off |
| Dirt roads by difficulty | Easy (green), medium (yellow), hard (red), not graded (brown), private or closed (black) |
| 3D buildings | In the 3D view, close in |
| Speed cameras | Icons with the limit |
| Weather | Wind, temperature, rain, UV ([more](weather.md)) |

## Detail by zoom

☰ → **Detail** sets from how close each kind of detail shows: each road class, rivers and
streams, water names, street names and road numbers. Choose **Very close**, **Close**,
**Middle**, **Far** or **Very far**. The many small details stop at Middle so the map stays
readable; a level is only offered where the map files hold that detail.

![Detail by zoom](../img/app/map-detail.png)

## Names, units, size and language

- ☰ → **Look** → **Names on the map**: in the app's language or as written locally.
- Settings → Preferences: **units** (km or miles, °C or °F, L/100 km, km/L, mpg UK or US; the
  defaults follow the phone's country), **interface size** from 85 % to 150 % for reading
  with a helmet on, and the **language** (English, Português, Español, Français, Italiano,
  Deutsch).
